# Privacy Policy for Unify

**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

## About Unify

Unify is a student-focused mobile application created to improve how students 
of **DII (dii.tj)** access and interact with their university portal. 
Our goal is simple: take the existing **portal.dii.tj** web experience and 
turn it into a faster, cleaner, more comfortable mobile application that 
students can rely on every day.

Instead of opening a browser, logging in, waiting for pages to load, and 
hunting through tabs, Unify brings everything you need from the portal 
directly into one organized mobile app, with a modern academic design 
built for student life.

## What Unify Offers

Unify is built as an improved mobile companion to portal.dii.tj. Features we 
provide or are building include:

- **Class notifications** — You will be notified before your next class 
  starts, so you never miss a lecture, lab, or seminar.
- **GPA tracking** — You can view your GPA and academic standing directly 
  inside the app at any time, without digging through the portal.
- **Attendance monitoring** — Keep an eye on your attendance records, see 
  which classes you've attended, and know exactly where you stand before 
  it becomes a problem.
- **Schedule overview** — See today's classes, tomorrow's classes, and 
  your full weekly schedule at a glance.
- **Stay updated** — Receive important academic updates, announcements, 
  and notifications from the university in a clean, organized feed.
- **Faster access** — Everything from the portal, reorganized for mobile, 
  with better speed and a better experience.

Our mission with Unify is to make student life at DII smoother, more 
organized, and more connected — without adding complexity.

## Data Collection

**We do not want to collect your personal data.**

Unify is designed as a reader and presenter of information that already 
belongs to you and already exists on the official DII portal 
(portal.dii.tj). We do not build profiles on students, we do not sell 
information, we do not share student data with advertisers, and we do not 
run any kind of tracking or profiling for commercial purposes.

The app works by connecting to the official DII portal using your own 
university login credentials. Any academic information (such as your 
schedule, GPA, attendance, or notifications) is retrieved from the 
university's own systems and shown to you inside the app. We do not store 
this information on separate servers for marketing or analytical purposes.

The only information handled by the app is what is strictly necessary for 
the app to work:

- Your university login credentials, used only to sign you in to the DII 
  portal, exactly as you would do in a web browser.
- Local device data such as notification preferences, saved on your device 
  so the app can remind you before classes.
- Basic technical information (such as crash reports) that may be used 
  only to fix bugs and improve stability.

We do not ask for data we don't need. We do not want data we don't need.
